Nightrooms is a solo-developed game made with Godot, combining original creative work with AI-assisted development.

Game Design & Programming

All gameplay mechanics, systems, and balancing rules were designed and written by me. AI tools were used throughout development to assist with programming, implementation, simulations, load testing, and other technical tasks.

While the game design and its underlying rules are my own, AI has been an important part of the development workflow, particularly when it comes to testing, iterating, and implementing systems as a solo developer.

Art & Visual Assets

Some of the game's visual assets were created with generative AI under my creative direction.

These assets are not necessarily final. One of my long-term goals is to improve and progressively replace AI-generated artwork through collaborations with human artists as the project grows and resources become available.

Music & Soundtrack

All musical compositions are original and written by me.

The soundtrack was produced using Strudel, with AI assistance for translating my compositions into code, implementing arrangements, and mixing.

Writing & Localization

The original Portuguese text was manually reviewed, while AI tools were used to assist with translations into other languages.

Nightrooms is an independent project developed with limited resources. AI has made it possible for me to build, test, and iterate on a much larger scope than I could reasonably manage alone.

My goal is to keep improving the game, collaborate with other creators whenever possible, and eventually replace temporary assets with artwork created specifically for Nightrooms.

The creative decisions, game mechanics, and overall direction remain my own.
